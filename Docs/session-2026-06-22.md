### CameraFit — the foundational scaling fix
- Root problem: the camera had a **hardcoded `orthographicSize = 5`** (no adaptation), and the canvas
  scaled by a width/height blend — so on any non-9:16 phone the HUD drifted from the playfield, and
  the 4 columns even **clipped** on taller phones.
- New `Core/CameraFit.cs` self-attaches to `Camera.main` and sizes ortho so the play area always
  **fills the screen width** (`max(DESIGN_ORTHO_SIZE, (PLAY_AREA_WIDTH/2)/aspect)`); canvas now
  **matches width** (`MATCH_WIDTH_OR_HEIGHT = 0`). Both frame by width → HUD + playfield scale in
  lockstep. No-op at the reference aspect. (Was extracted to a `camera-fit` branch, tested, then
  cherry-picked back.)

## Conventions reinforced (now habits)
- **Aspect-preserving sizing**: size sprites by a target **height**; width = `height × sprite.aspect`.
  Forcing a **square** (icons) or a **fixed Vector2** (title tab) distorts non-square art — that was
  the "trophy/star look weird and pixelated" bug. Used for top-bar icons, the title tab, the SPECIAL
  ORDER banner, and the special-order ingredient stack.
- **Baked art ⇒ display at native aspect** or the halftone dots smear. Exception: the Special Order
  card is **intentionally stretched** taller (a completed burger) — accepted per dev.
- **Uncompressed import** (`textureCompression: 0`) for fine-detail icons shown small — DXT block
  artifacts otherwise (currency icons).
- **Screen-space UGUI for all HUD** so it scales with the camera (both frame by width).
