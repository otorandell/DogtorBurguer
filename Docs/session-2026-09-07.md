## 2. Feel fixes from the phone (all in code, playtest-confirmed direction, retest pending)
- **30 fps cap**: nothing set `Application.targetFrameRate`, Unity's mobile default is 30.
  `AppBootstrap.ApplyFrameRate` now targets the display refresh, floor `Constants.MIN_TARGET_FRAME_RATE` (60).
- **Gesture latency** (`Input/TouchInputHandler.cs`, new `Input/PressPhase.cs` enum
  None/Open/SwipeOnly): swipes fire when the finger crosses the threshold, not on lift; in
  **Tap mode the whole tap intent resolves on the PRESS** (side-move, swap, fast-drop, preview,
  fairy) and the press goes SwipeOnly; Drag mode keeps taps on the lift (a press on the chef or
  a piece there is usually a swipe start). Touch + mouse share one Begin/Continue/End lifecycle.
- **Chef retargets mid-move**: the `_isMoving` early-return in `ChefController.MoveToPosition`
  dropped any swipe during the 0.15 s tween; removed (SwapPlates keeps its guard).

## 3. Shop on tall phones
`ShopScreen` anchored the X, the top bar and the scroll viewport to the CANVAS while the page art
is a fixed 540×960 sheet — on a 20:9 phone the chrome floated above the awning and cells scrolled
outside the page. Fixed with a page-root rect at `REFERENCE_RESOLUTION` (the ModalPanel pattern);
rule recorded in CLAUDE.md: full-canvas-sheet screens root their content on the page, never the canvas.

## 4. Rulesets — the arc (important context for the tables)
1. Oscar preferred the old **Relax** pacing (3× thresholds) as the default. First pass: swapped
   Classic/Relax → Classic/Hard with per-mode tables, per-mode trophies and two Play Games boards.
2. Then decided **one ruleset only**: `GameMode` deleted entirely, plus per-mode tables, the
   card's red CLASSIC/RELAX tab, per-mode high scores/boards (back to ONE `highScore` key and
   ONE leaderboard id). Speed players pick a higher START level instead.
3. **Special Orders**: the old size-only orders (`OrderType.Size`, the "N" single placeholder)
   are gone; every order is an exact-count recipe from `ORDER_SIZE_BY_LEVEL` /
   `ORDER_NAMED_BY_LEVEL` (Oscar's slow ladder: 1 named until L4, 2 until L7, then +1 named
   every ~4 levels; total +1 every 2 levels; 11-7 by L21) with one "?" mystery slot PER free
   ingredient on the card.
4. **Pacing is time-budgeted**: a placement count is a bad clock (pieces fall ~4× faster at
   L20). `LEVEL_THRESHOLDS` is derived so each level lasts ~**42 s** of play
   (`scratchpad/level_time.py`: secPerPlacement ≈ (9 rows × fallStep + WAVE_MOVE) / (2 +
   tripleChance); started at 60 s, trimmed 30% the same day). L20 at 917 placements, kill screen
   1012, ~14 min. vs the original table: early levels +25…70% pieces, late levels ~3.5×.
   **Re-derive whenever the fall or triple tables change.**

## 5. Settings: START level row
The menu's third row is now a player feature replacing the mode toggle: the blue blank with a
yellow `ui_arrow_yellow` button INSIDE each end and "START: LVL N" between
(`SettingsPanel.BuildLevelRow`; knobs `SETTINGS_LEVEL_ARROW_X/_H`; arrows hide at the range
ends). The old flat placeholder stepper + the `MainMenuUI` Show Level Stepper opt-in are gone.
Open: `SETTINGS_LEVEL_CAP` is still `KILLER_LEVEL` for testing — lower it (maybe well below 20)
before release; a run started high competes on the same leaderboard as one from L1 (accepted for now).
