# Economy study — 2026-09-05

Money/time valuation of both currencies, run before the pre-launch balance pass. All source
values in `MonetizationConfig` (+ per-skin `_gemCost`/`_starCost`). The retunes at the bottom
were applied the same day.

## Conversion anchors

- Gems: $0.99/100 (0.99 c/gem) down to $19.99/2600 (0.77 c/gem) — ladder monotonically improves.
- Gems to stars: 40/200 (5.0), 100/550 (5.5), 200/1200 (6.0 stars per gem).
- Chained: **1 star = 0.13-0.20 cents** (about 500 stars per dollar at best rates).

## Star income per run (model)

- Orders: 3 + 2(lvl-1) each; cumulative 6 through challenge L1, 21 L2, 49 L3, 94 L4.
- Score payout: score / 500.
- Star fairies: 1.2 fairies/min x 20% stars x 25 = ~6 stars/min EV (gems: ~1.2 gems/min EV).
- Personas: casual (~4 min, mid-L3, ~3k pts) ~50-60 stars; good (~8 min, L4, ~10k pts) ~130-160.

## Decisions applied 2026-09-05

1. **FREE gems rung capped at 3/day** (`GEM_AD_DAILY_CAP`; SaveDataManager persists date+count).
   Uncapped, 4 rewarded views equalled the $0.99 pack at ~1-2 c/view actual ad revenue.
2. **Gem-cheap skins 30 -> 60 gems, chef European 50 -> 80**: at 30 the premium tier was cheaper
   than the 400-star free tier on every axis (money, time, effort) — broken price signal.
3. **Consumables 150/400/1000 -> 100/270/700 stars**: 3 casual games per single use invites
   hoarding; ~2 games per use keeps them circulating (they drive the fun loop).
4. **Left alone deliberately**: fairy-heavy star income (rewards active play), the 5000/10000-star
   prestige grinds, continue pricing, both pack ladders, Remove Ads pricing.
5. **Remove Ads banner recomposed from widgets** (was the baked-values mock art) so the pill can
   show the store's localized price — see ShopSections.BuildSupportBanner.

Revisit once real runs generate data (run length, score, orders/run are assumptions here).
