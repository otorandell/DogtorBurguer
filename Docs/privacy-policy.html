Privacy Policy — Dogtor Burguer

Effective date: 1 September 2026

Dogtor Burguer ("the Game") is developed and published by ProximaCentaury (Oscar Torandell) ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data is processed when you play the Game.

Data we collect directly

None. The Game stores your progress (scores, settings, in-game currency, unlocked items and purchases) locally on your device. We do not operate servers, require accounts, or receive your data. Uninstalling the Game deletes this local data.

Data collected by third-party services

The Game includes the following third-party services, which process data to provide their function:

Advertising — Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) and mediated ad networks

To serve ads, the SDK and its partner networks may collect: the device advertising identifier (GAID on Android / IDFA on iOS), IP address (coarse location), device model and OS version, and ad-interaction data (views, clicks). This is used for ad delivery, frequency capping, fraud prevention and — with your consent where required — ad personalization.

  • Unity / ironSource privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalization in your device

settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).

Purchases — Google Play / Apple App Store (via Unity In-App Purchasing)

In-app purchases are processed entirely by the store. We never see your payment details; the Game only receives a confirmation that a purchase succeeded so it can unlock the item. The store's privacy policy applies to the transaction, and Unity's policy (link above) applies to the purchasing library.

Consent (EEA / UK) and tracking (iOS)

Where required by law (GDPR / ePrivacy), a consent prompt is shown before personalized ads are served; declining still shows non-personalized ads. On iOS, the App Tracking Transparency prompt governs cross-app tracking. You can change your choice at any time from the Game's Settings.

Children

The Game is a general-audience arcade game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children; the advertising SDK is configured accordingly.

Your rights

Depending on your region you may have rights to access, delete, or object to the processing of your personal data. Because we hold no data ourselves, exercise these rights with the ad provider (link above) — or contact us and we will point you the right way.

Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above changes when we do. Material changes will be noted in a Game update's release notes.
