Dogtor Burger ("the Game") is developed by [DEVELOPER NAME] ("we", "us"). This policy
explains what data is processed when you play the Game.

## Data we collect directly

**None.** The Game stores your progress (scores, settings, in-game currency and purchases)
locally on your device. We do not operate servers, require accounts, or receive your data.

## Data collected by third-party services

The Game includes the following third-party SDKs, which process data to provide their service:

### Advertising — Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) and mediated ad networks
To serve ads, the SDK and its partner networks may collect: device advertising identifier
(GAID/IDFA), IP address (coarse location), device model and OS version, and ad-interaction
data (views, clicks). This is used for ad delivery, frequency capping, fraud prevention and —
with your consent where required — ad personalization.

- Unity/ironSource privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
- You can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalization in your
  device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy → Tracking).

### Purchases — Google Play / Apple App Store
In-app purchases are processed entirely by the store. We never see your payment details.
The store's privacy policy applies to the transaction.

## Consent (EEA/UK) and tracking (iOS)

Where required by law (GDPR/ePrivacy), a consent prompt is shown before personalized ads are
served; declining still shows non-personalized ads. On iOS, the App Tracking Transparency
prompt governs cross-app tracking.

## Children
