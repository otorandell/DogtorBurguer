## Architectural decisions made

- **6-config-file architecture** (F-16): split by "who would tune this." Adopted Option B (medium-grained, 6 files including new `MonetizationConfig` and `AudioConfig`).
- **No nested types** (project rule, added to CLAUDE.md): overrides global "nested types are fine" allowance. Promote any nested struct/class/enum to its own top-level file. Drives `BurgerData` promotion (F-26), `MatchResult` promotion (F-32).
- **Paired identical-value constants kept independent**: AnimConfig pairs (`POPUP_RISE` / `FLOATING_TEXT_RISE`, etc.) and `Constants.MAX_INGREDIENT_COUNT` / `GameplayConfig.CHALLENGE_MAX_SIZE` — designer wants independent tuning surface.
- **`Paused` is a modifier, not a state** (F-21): refactor to `private bool _isPaused` on GameManager; GameState enum becomes `{Menu, Playing, GameOver}`.
- **Position bubbles: visible UX cue** (F-9): raise alpha + change sortingOrder rather than delete.
- **Procedural audio: option A** (F-2): per-clip `[SerializeField]` override fields, procedural fallback. Path to authored audio assets without losing procedural baseline.
- **Game-input freeze during burger resolution** (F-31): global lock with pause as the only exception (pause takes priority over everything else).
- **SaveDataManager production-readiness** (out of review scope): tracked separately in `Docs/pre-launch-checklist.md`.
